Source: supplied IM77 Frequency Tables PDF (private; source sha256 ce3f5e3c...dfe94), physical pages 1-8, each read directly from the rendered page image.
Corpus: Night Shift `query_data` (source id `indus-corpus-v1`).

## What these pages are

Physical pages 1-8 are not single-sign totals. They are the table headed "Doublets frequencies greater than 0" (2730 records in all). Each page lists 24 sign pairs with a count, so pages 1-8 cover the 192 most frequent pairs.

Method: for each printed pair `a b` I ran `query_data sequence [b, a]`. The corpus stores signs in the reverse of display order (S1 first). This was checked: printed 328-89 = 124; `sequence [89,328]` = 124; `sequence [328,89]` = 0. The comparison uses `matches` (occurrences), not `texts`.

Difference = corpus - printed. Percent = |difference| / printed.

Notes on cause:
- Misread print: unlikely. The digits on these pages are clean, and the neighbouring cells (same sign, nearby counts) match exactly.
- Different corpus edition: the corpus is always higher and never lower, which fits a later or expanded edition (the corpus is the 2021 IM77/IDF80 web application). This has not been shown text by text, so the cause is recorded as unknown.
- Sign split or merge: no evidence. Each of these signs also appears in other pairs on these pages that match exactly.
- Five of the six larger differences are +1 or +2 on counts of 17 or less. Each crosses the 5% line only because the count is small.
