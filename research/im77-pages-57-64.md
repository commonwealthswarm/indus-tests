## Method

Source pages are headed Doublets frequencies greater than 0. Singleton sign queries proposed by the task measure a different statistic. A displayed left-to-right pair A-B was queried via op sequence with signs [B,A], as corpus metadata explicitly states that stored order reverses published display. This is not an inference of reading direction. Source ID: indus-corpus-v1. Counts use returned sequence matches. No singleton token-frequency claim is made.

Difference = corpus minus printed; relative difference = absolute difference / printed total * 100. The full per-page comparison CSVs (columns physical_page, sign_number, printed_total, corpus_count, difference) are kept only in the task's Night Shift lab notes #331-#338, because they transcribe the private source; they are deliberately not committed to this repository.
