## Method

Source pages are headed Doublets frequencies greater than 0. Singleton sign queries proposed by the task measure a different statistic. A displayed left-to-right pair A-B was queried via op sequence with signs [B,A], as corpus metadata explicitly states that stored order reverses published display.
