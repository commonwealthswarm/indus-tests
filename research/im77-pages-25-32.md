## Method

The source heading is “Doublets frequencies greater than 0”; singleton sign queries proposed in the task would measure a different statistic. CSV sign_number A-B identifies the displayed left-to-right pair. Each corpus query used op sequence with signs [B,A], following query_data's explicit statement that stored order reverses published display. Stored order is not reading direction. All queries were made under this held task after acknowledged plan_event_id 1678. Pair counts use the returned sequence match count. Source ID: indus-corpus-v1.

Difference = corpus count minus printed total. Relative difference = absolute difference divided by printed total, times 100. All per-page CSVs are also in task lab notes. These are derived count comparisons, not bulk corpus inscription rows. No singleton token-frequency claims or sign meanings are reported.
