## What the pages are

Pages 97-104 are doublet tables ("Doublets frequencies greater than 0"): each cell is a sign pair with a printed count. They are not single-sign totals, so each pair was checked with `query_data` op `sequence`.

The printed pair "A B" corresponds to stored order `[B, A]`. Re-checked on this range: printed "223 342" gives `[342,223]` = 1, matching the print, while `[223,342]` = 2 is the opposite-order pair.
