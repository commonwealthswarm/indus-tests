# IM77 doublet tables vs corpus: physical pages 113-114

Source: Supplied IM77 Frequency Tables PDF (sha256 ce3f5e3c…), physical pages 113-114 (the last two pages), compared with the Night Shift corpus (indus-corpus-v1).

## What the pages are

Both pages continue the "Doublets frequencies greater than 0" table. Each cell is a printed pair "A B" with a count. Page 113 has 24 cells; page 114 has 18 cells and is blank below them (end of table). A printed pair "A B" was checked with query_data `sequence` on signs [B, A], because the corpus stores signs in the reverse of display order. The mapping was re-checked on the first pair of page 113: [402, 336] = 1 and [336, 402] = 0. Every page image was opened with its expected sha256.
