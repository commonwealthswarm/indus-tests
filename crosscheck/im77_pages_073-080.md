## What the pages are

Pages 73-80 are doublet tables ("Doublets frequencies greater than 0"): each cell is a sign pair with a printed count. They are not single-sign totals, so each pair was checked with `query_data` op `sequence`.

The printed pair "A B" corresponds to stored order `[B, A]` (the corpus stores S1 first and the display reverses it). Checked e.g. `[244,245]` = 1 vs `[245,244]` = 0, `[79,343]` = 1 vs `[343,79]` = 0.
