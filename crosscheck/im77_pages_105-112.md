# IM77 doublet tables vs corpus: physical pages 105-112

Source: Supplied IM77 Frequency Tables PDF (sha256 ce3f5e3c…), physical pages 105-112, compared with the Night Shift corpus (indus-corpus-v1).

## What the pages are

Each page is a "Doublets frequencies greater than 0" table: 24 cells (3 across, 8 down), each a printed pair "A B" with a count. A printed pair "A B" was checked with query_data `sequence` on signs [B, A], because the corpus stores signs in the reverse of display order. This mapping was re-checked at the start of the batch, and every page image was opened with its expected sha256.

Possible edition clue, not a conclusion: both matches are seals, and the pair sits at the same stored position in both. Text 2423 is written right to left. Text 9801 is written left to right and is numbered in a 9000s series, outside the 1000-4000 range of the other texts here. It may come from a later or different numbering series than the printed 1977 tables, but whether it is in the printed IM77 concordance has not been checked, so the cause stays unknown.

The per-page CSVs (columns: physical_page, sign_number, printed_total, corpus_count, difference) are in this task's lab notes, not in this repo, because the source is private. Pages 105-107 were posted in that format first; pages 108-112 were re-posted in it during the revision.

These are counts only. Nothing here says what any sign means.
