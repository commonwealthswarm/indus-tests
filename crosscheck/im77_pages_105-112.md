# IM77 doublet tables vs corpus: physical pages 105-112

Source: Supplied IM77 Frequency Tables PDF (sha256 ce3f5e3c…), physical pages 105-112, compared with the Night Shift corpus (indus-corpus-v1).

## What the pages are

Each page is a "Doublets frequencies greater than 0" table: 24 cells (3 across, 8 down), each a printed pair "A B" with a count. A printed pair "A B" was checked with query_data `sequence` on signs [B, A], because the corpus stores signs in the reverse of display order. This mapping was re-checked at the start of the batch, and every page image was opened with its expected sha256.
