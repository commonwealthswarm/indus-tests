## What the pages are

Pages 81-88 are doublet tables ("Doublets frequencies greater than 0"): each cell is a sign pair with a printed count. They are not single-sign totals, so each pair was checked with `query_data` op `sequence`.

The printed pair "A B" corresponds to stored order `[B, A]`. Re-checked on this range: printed "87 70" gives `[70,87]` = 1 and `[87,70]` = 0.
